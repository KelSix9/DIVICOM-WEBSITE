Patented Innovation

The ClipPlus.c Technology System

"Clip, Fill & Connect" — High-precision serial construction simplified into 3 steps.

01

1. Easy Clipping System

Flexible combination of patented uPVC profiles that snap together effortlessly. Eliminates the need for plastering or painting entirely.

02

2. Simple Connections

Integrated preinstalled conduits for water, sewage, and electricity. No wall slitting, chiselling, or messy site trenching required.

03

3. Local Material Infill

Profiles are filled with locally-sourced building materials (reinforced concrete, sand, or recycled aggregate) matching regional standards.
